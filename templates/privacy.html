Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. When you use Spendly to track your finances, we collect the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide the features of the app: storing your expenses, generating spending summaries and category breakdowns, and letting you sign in to access your records across sessions.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not share your expense data with third-party services. Any third-party tools used to operate the application, such as hosting or infrastructure providers, only process data as needed to keep the service running and do not have independent access to your financial records.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach out to us through the contact details provided on this site.
